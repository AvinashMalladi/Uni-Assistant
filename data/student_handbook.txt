=== ATTENDANCE POLICY (Section 3.7) ===
A minimum of 75% attendance in each course is mandatory to be eligible for the End Semester Examination (ESE). Attendance is calculated course-wise.
- If attendance >= 75%: Eligible for exams directly.
- If attendance is between 65% and 75%: Student must pay a condonation fee and submit supporting documents; the Condonation Board reviews the case and decides eligibility.
- If attendance is below 65%: Student is DETAINED from the course (awarded 'R' grade) and must re-register for the course in a later semester or the Summer Semester.
Permitted absence can be used for medical emergencies, family functions, or approved University activities (seminars, NSS/NCC camps, sports, cultural events).

=== GRADING SYSTEM (Section 3.8) ===
Letter Grade | Performance | Marks Range | Grade Point
A+ | Excellent | 86-100 | 10
A | Very Good | 76-85 | 8.5
B+ | Good | 66-75 | 7.5
B | Above Average | 51-65 | 6
C | Average | 45-50 | 5
R | Repeat (inadequate attendance/Dropped/Unregistered) | - | 0
F | Fail/Malpractice | - | 0
I | Incomplete (exam fee not paid/absent in end exam) | - | 0

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) = (Sum of Credits x Grade Points for each course) / (Sum of Credits)
CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) = weighted average of SGPA across all completed semesters, weighted by credits per semester.
Formula for conversion of CGPA to percentage of marks: Percentage = 10 x CGPA.
Minimum passing requirement: 45% aggregate marks for Undergraduate programs; 60% aggregate marks for Postgraduate and PhD programs.
When a student repeats a course, the BETTER grade is considered for CGPA. The 'I' grade is not considered while calculating CGPA.

=== EXAMINATION PROCESS AND GRADE RECOVERY (Section 3.9) ===
Eligibility to appear for exams requires: registration for the course, completed exam registration, paid exam fee, and meeting minimum attendance.
- Closed Book Examination: no reference material allowed, tests conceptual understanding.
- Open Book Examination: approved learning resources allowed, tests application and analysis.
- Mid-Term Make-up Examination: available for students absent or scoring below qualifying marks in the mid-term, for a fee.
- Grade Improvement: Students with A, B+, B, or C grades may re-register for Grade Improvement once per course before graduation; the better of the two grades is retained for CGPA, but revised grades are NOT considered for university medals or ranks.
- Clearing F Grade: One Supplementary Examination attempt allowed; maximum grade awardable through supplementary is C.
- Clearing R Grade: Must re-register and complete all evaluation components again (Summer Semester or next offering).
- Clearing I Grade: May appear for End Semester Make-up Examination after paying the fee; marks replace the original End Semester marks.
Declaration of Results: Published via SRAAP portal after approval by Dean Academics. Correction requests must be submitted within 15 days of result declaration.

=== PROMOTION POLICY (Section 3.10, B.Tech - Flowchart 2.4a) ===
- End of First Year: Promoted to Second Year if CGPA >= 4.50 OR Credits >= 40%. Otherwise, may continue at own risk for max 2 semesters (medical grounds) or repeat the first year. Note: only ONE chance is given during the entire UG program to continue at own risk in this way.
- End of Second Year: Promoted to Third Year if CGPA >= 5.00 OR Credits >= 40%. Otherwise on-hold, not promoted, must register for failed courses whenever offered.
- End of Third Year: Promoted to Fourth Year if CGPA >= 5.00 OR Credits >= 40%.

Promotion Policy for BBA/BCA (Flowchart 2.4b):
- End of First Year: Promoted to Second Year if CGPA >= 4.50 OR Credits >= 40%.
- End of Second Year: Promoted to Third Year if CGPA >= 4.50 OR Credits >= 50%.

Students admitted through lateral entry are governed by promotion requirements applicable from the second year onwards.

=== DEGREE AWARD REQUIREMENTS AND HONORS (Section 3.17) ===
Minimum CGPA for UG degree award: 6.00 or above, plus completion of all courses, compliance with academic rules, and clearance of all dues.
- Chancellor's Gold Medal: best UG student overall, minimum 9.0 CGPA, no backlogs, no disciplinary case.
- Vice-Chancellor's Medal: top performer per program/branch, minimum 8.5 CGPA, no backlogs, no disciplinary case.
- Semester Topper Award: highest gamification + academic points per semester, minimum 8.0 CGPA.

=== HONOURS AND MINOR DEGREE (Section 3.4) ===
Honours Degree: minimum 16 credits of approved advanced courses via overloading (regular or Summer Semester); must maintain prescribed minimum CGPA.
Minor Degree: minimum 16 credits from an approved Minor basket, distinct from core curriculum, via overloading.

=== ACADEMIC INTEGRITY / ANTI-PLAGIARISM (Annexure I) ===
Plagiarism Penalty Levels (for theses/publications):
- Level 0: Similarity up to 10% (single source) - No penalty if under 1%; thesis passes as per regulations.
- Level 1: Similarity 10-20% - Scholar must revise and resubmit within 30 days (one chance only).
- Level 2: Similarity above 20% - Formal penalty per regulations and UGC norms; may be barred for one year from submitting academic documents.
Academic Integrity Committee (AIC) and School Academic Integrity Committee (SAIC) handle investigations.
