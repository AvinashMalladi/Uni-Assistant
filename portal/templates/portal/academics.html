Academic Regulations

Attendance

Minimum 75% attendance is required in every course to be eligible for the End Semester Examination. Between 65-75% requires condonation approval; below 65% results in detention.

Grading

Letter grades range from A+ (10 grade points) down to C (5 grade points), with R/F/I grades for detained, failed, or incomplete courses respectively.

Promotion

Promotion to the next year requires meeting a minimum CGPA or credit-completion threshold, checked at the end of each academic year.
